## 1. Voice, hashtags and the messaging checklist

### 1.1 Voice and tone

- **Professional and human.** Write as Paxofi Technologies LTD speaking to capable people at the start of their careers: warm, direct, respectful. The approved brand values are *People First, Innovation Always, Real Solutions, Transforming Tomorrow*; the approved sign-off is *"Transforming tomorrow, together."* (Brand Guidelines v1.0, updated 15 Oct 2026). Corporate tagline: *Innovating Today. Transforming Tomorrow.* PIF headline: *Build. Learn. Contribute.*
- **Clear before clever.** Say what PIF is (a remote, structured, unpaid learning programme), who each engagement type is for, the hours and the dates. One idea per sentence.
- **Development-oriented, no hype.** Talk about real project experience, structured learning in the Paxofi Academy, a supervisor and mentor, feedback and a certificate on meeting the requirements. Do not use "life-changing", "dream job", "guaranteed", "career launchpad", "hiring", "vacancy", "salary" or "stipend".
- **Inclusive.** Welcome students, recent graduates, SIWES/industrial-training students, career changers and self-taught people from anywhere in Nigeria. Do not imply a university degree is required. Never ask for or mention age beyond "18 or older", or anything about religion, ethnicity, finances or politics.
- **Local and honest.** Lagos-based company, remote programme: "wherever you are in Nigeria, as long as you have reliable internet." Use Lagos time (WAT) for deadlines.
- **British English** in body text (programme, organisation, colour). Keep the official public role titles exactly as published, including *Program Coordinator Fellow*.

### 1.2 Hashtags (proposed — no approved hashtag set exists in Notion)

Use two or three per post, at the end. Never use #Hiring, #Jobs, #JobAlert or #Vacancy (PIF is not employment).

### 1.3 Messaging checklist — tick every item before a post goes out

From the Final Launch Production Pack §2 (mandatory public messaging rules) and D-027 / `PIF-CAMPAIGN-PLAN.md` §4.

- [ ] **Engagement type named with the role**: "Frontend Developer Intern", never just "Frontend Developer" or "opportunity".
- [ ] **Unpaid learning programme; not employment.** No promise of a stipend, allowance, job offer or guaranteed outcome. No "job", "hiring", "salaried" or "employment" language.
- [ ] **Remote; 18 or older; the weekly hours**: Fellows and Interns at least 15 (target 20); Volunteers at least 10 (target 15).
- [ ] **Dates right**: applications open Mon 2 Nov 2026; Cohort 1 = applications by Sun 15 Nov; applications close Sun 29 Nov; Cohort 1 starts Mon 7 Dec 2026. No other deadlines invented.
- [ ] **Formal decisions only by email from hr@paxofi.com.** WhatsApp is for reminders and support only, never decisions.
- [ ] **"Limited places — Cohort 1"**, never seat numbers.
- [ ] **No mentor, supervisor or other internal names**; no internal stage numbers, register names or status words (e.g. "Agreement Pending").
- [ ] **The exact tracked link** for this channel and role from §3 (copied, not retyped). Open it once to check the page loads.
- [ ] Creative uses the public candidate journey only (PIF-DGM-001, diagram 2) and Paxofi brand tokens; correct dates on the image as well as in the caption.
- [ ] Recorded in the "which link went where" list (`PIF-CAMPAIGN-PLAN.md` §4).

**Short posts (X, Instagram stories, WhatsApp).** Where space is tight, the post still carries the type, "unpaid, not employment", remote, 18+ and the date; the role page behind the tracked link carries the full terms.

- Teasers (T01, T02) only after the staff-area dates in `PIF-CAMPAIGN-PLAN.md` §2 are corrected, so careers.paxofi.com says applications open on Monday 2 November.

## 5. FAQ for replies to comments

Keep replies short, friendly and factual. Move personal questions to email. Never discuss an individual application in public or on WhatsApp.

**Is it paid?**
> Thanks for asking. PIF is an unpaid learning and development programme. It is not employment, and there is no promise of pay or a job. What you get is real project experience, structured learning in the Paxofi Academy, a supervisor and mentor, feedback and a certificate on meeting the programme requirements.

**Do I get a certificate?**
> Yes, on meeting the programme requirements: a Certificate of Completion for Fellows, an Internship Completion Certificate for Interns and a Certificate of Volunteer Service for Volunteers. A reference letter is available on request.

**Is it remote? Do I need to be in Lagos?**
> It is fully remote, so you can take part from anywhere in Nigeria. You need reliable internet and the tools for your role.

**How many hours a week?**
> Fellows and Interns: at least 15 hours a week (target 20). Volunteers: at least 10 (target 15).

**How long is it?**
> Fellows: 24 programme weeks. Interns: 12 or 24 weeks. Volunteers: 8 to 24 weeks, depending on the project. Cohort 1 starts on Monday 7 December 2026.

**What's the difference between Fellow, Intern and Volunteer?**
> Fellows have foundation skills in the role and own real deliverables. Interns are students, recent graduates or early-career learners; potential matters more than experience. Volunteers contribute to a defined project. Each role page says which types it is offered as.

**Can SIWES / IT students apply?**
> Yes, SIWES and industrial-training students are welcome as PIF Interns. If your institution needs paperwork for the placement, mention it in your application or email hr@paxofi.com.

**Who can apply? Is there an age limit?**
> Anyone aged 18 or over who can commit the weekly hours and work remotely.

**How do I apply?**
> Choose a role and apply on careers.paxofi.com: [paste the tracked home link for the channel you are replying on]. You will get an acknowledgement email when your application arrives.

**What's the deadline?**
> Apply by Sunday 15 November to be considered for Cohort 1. Applications close on Sunday 29 November; applications received after 15 November are considered for the talent pool and a future cohort.

**How does selection work?**
> For Fellows and Interns: a review of your application and evidence, a short role task and an interview. For Volunteers: a short conversation. Our tasks never ask for work Paxofi intends to use.

**When will I hear back? Can you tell me my status here?**
